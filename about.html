🎯 Project Overview

This system predicts crop, yield and production using Machine Learning and comprehensive agricultural dataset. Our AI-powered platform helps farmers make data-driven decisions for optimal crop selection and resource management.

By analyzing key parameters like soil nutrients (N-P-K), climate conditions (rainfall, temperature), and seasonal patterns, we provide accurate predictions that maximize agricultural productivity.

Our mission is to empower farmers with technology, reduce crop failure risks, and contribute to sustainable agriculture practices.
